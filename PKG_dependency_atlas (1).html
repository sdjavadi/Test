Needs = can't be done (or validated) without it. Improves = works without it, better with it. “Ready now” = every hard prerequisite, followed through the chain, exists today (product holdings count as available for TM products only).

Table links: feeds = rows or values come from it; lookup = joins ids, names or attributes from it; check = only decides whether or when a month may be built. Names of tables still to make are not decided unless shown in code. Gates are working guesses, not a compliance read.
