Subject: neo4j_payments: duplicated inbound RTP transactions (5C/6C) and a few data questions

Hi team,

While building Payment Knowledge Graph metrics from dsihd01p_dsi.neo4j_payments, we found inbound RTP payments recorded twice. We also have a few questions about fields and semantics.

1) Duplicate inbound RTP payments (5C / 6C)
Every month from 2024-01 to 2026-08, about 1.05–1.75M rows in 6C.RTP_P2P_CPTY_PAYS (~21% of 6C) duplicate a row in 5C.RTP_PRT_CPTY_PAYS. Receiving account, amount, date and counterparty name all match (names agree on 99.9% of matches). The two copies differ in trans_id, unq_cpty_acct_id, cpty_type (CPTY vs P2P_CPTY) and src_syst (PRT vs RTP), so the same counterparty becomes two different nodes in the graph. 6C never carries cpty_fin_entity_name; 5C does.

2) One-day date shift that follows daylight saving time
Some 6C copies are dated one day after their 5C twin. This happens only during DST: about 22–46K pairs a month from mid-March to early November, and near zero from December to February, every year 2024–2026. It looks like one feed converts timestamps with a fixed UTC offset, so late-evening payments land on the next day.

Example queries (Impala):

-- a) same-day duplicates on one date
SELECT a.trans_dt, a.pnc_dep_acct_receives, a.trans_amt, a.cpty_name,
       a.trans_id AS trans_id_5c, b.trans_id AS trans_id_6c,
       a.unq_cpty_acct_id AS cpty_acct_5c, b.unq_cpty_acct_id AS cpty_acct_6c,
       a.cpty_fin_entity_name AS fi_5c, b.cpty_fin_entity_name AS fi_6c,
       a.cpty_type AS type_5c, b.cpty_type AS type_6c,
       a.src_syst AS src_5c, b.src_syst AS src_6c
FROM dsihd01p_dsi.neo4j_payments a
JOIN dsihd01p_dsi.neo4j_payments b
  ON  b.pnc_dep_acct_receives = a.pnc_dep_acct_receives
  AND b.trans_amt = a.trans_amt
  AND upper(trim(b.cpty_name)) = upper(trim(a.cpty_name))
WHERE a.category = '5C.RTP_PRT_CPTY_PAYS' AND a.trans_dt = '2025-01-15'
  AND b.category = '6C.RTP_P2P_CPTY_PAYS' AND b.trans_dt = '2025-01-15'
LIMIT 20;

3) Other gaps
- cpty_name is 100% null in 7C.CHECK_CPTY_PAYS.
- src_syst is empty for all ACH, check and PCARD categories.

Questions
1. Do the PRT and P2P feeds capture the same RTP payments? If so, which is the system of record, and can the duplicate be removed upstream or in the Neo4j ingestion?
2. Which feed has the DST date shift, and what time zone should trans_dt reflect?
3. Is there a source that links the P2P counterparty tokens in 6C to the account ids used in 5C?
4. How is unq_cpty_id constructed? It appears to include the counterparty name.
5. What do np_key and mdm_id_pays_orig / mdm_id_receives_orig represent (id at transaction time vs. after MDM merges)?
6. Is trans_amt converted to USD for rows where trans_currency is not USD?
7. How are returns and reversals (ACH returns, returned checks, wire recalls) represented, if at all?
8. For outbound checks (7B), is the bank-of-first-deposit routing number available from the presentment data, so cpty_fin_entity_name could be populated?
9. Is history in neo4j_payments retained permanently, or are older partitions purged?

We have a pairing table (each duplicate 6C trans_id matched to its 5C trans_id, 2024-01 → 2026-08) in bdahd01p_dlcdi1_cdi_tm.pkg_dedup_5c6c if it helps you validate.
