Subject: TPPP look-alikes and prospects: pre-read for Thursday

Hi all,

Ahead of Thursday, here is a short summary and a few questions I'd like your input on.

What I did
Using payment data from January to August 2026, I described every TM business client and every outside company our clients transact with by how their money moves (payers, payees, regularity, money in versus out, banks on the other side), and compared them with our ~100 registered Third-Party Payment/Payroll Processors.

How reliable it is
When I hid registered TPPPs from the method, 32 of 72 came back in the top 1% of about 70,000 business clients; random review would find fewer than one. For outside companies, hidden processor accounts came back in the top 1% about 20 times more often than random.

What came out
1. Clients behaving like TPPPs but not registered, each showing which registered TPPPs it resembles (payroll processors, collection agencies, benefits and claims administrators).
2. Outside processors we could win, such as Clio Payments, Vanco, Actum Processing and Intuit Payroll, each with the TM clients who already pay them.
3. Registered TPPPs whose payments from our own clients land mostly at other banks.

These are patterns worth checking, not conclusions about any company.

Questions for Thursday
1. Which kinds of TPPP matter most: payroll, merchant/payment processing, collections, or benefits and claims administration?
2. What makes a prospect worth pursuing: size of the flows with our clients, number of our clients involved, or new to bank only?
3. Should clients that declare a payment-processing industry code but are not registered be part of the review?
4. Are there other sources I could use to confirm candidates (KYC questionnaires, ACH originator data, a business-information subscription)?

On Thursday I'll walk through the top names on each list and show a prototype review app.
