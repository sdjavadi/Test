Three of these need active correction, not just retirement

1. fin_out_n has been briefed as "the number of banks a client pays" at 30× lift. It is the number of distinct institutions a client sends ACH to, it scores 42% of the book, and it ranks fifth of eight across the whole population.
2. "Combining signals doesn't help" should be withdrawn outright, not amended.
3. "Payments lead the balance by 2 months" is an event-time claim. The calendar-time replacement is a median lead of 2 months at a 250-name list and 4 months at incumbent volume. They are different measurements and the brief should carry the second.

6.3 What is still open

Competitor loss versus business contraction

Unanswered. The first attempt built a two-axis split — counterparty survival against ticket-hold — and returned a clean null: the stayer-median survival cut came out at 1.000, meaning essentially every baseline counterparty of a typical stayer is still being paid by someone. The axis is degenerate, both "partners gone" quadrants are empty, and the two surviving quadrants sit at lift 1.07 and 0.93. Only 1,150 of ~7,600 attriters could be classified.

Two changes would give it a chance, and both are cheap:

  • Raise the liveness bar from "any other payer" to at least two other payers, excluding the target.
  • Restrict the baseline set to counterparties with a low PNC payer count — a processor tells you nothing, a regional supplier tells you everything. One histogram of payer counts decides whether a cut leaves enough counterparties per client to compute a share over.

There is also a construct that has not been tried and needs no new data: a payment to a counterparty carrying the client's own name at a named non-PNC institution. Departing clients already route 1.7× more of their outflow to themselves (8.2% against 4.9%), and attaching cpty_fin_entity_name turns that trait into a named destination. The v8 version of this was too sparse to survive the dd transform (8.4% coverage); read as a level rather than a dd it may work.

The dd construction is now the coverage constraint

74.3% of client-months carry a fin_out_n value. Only 31% carry a dd. The transform costs 43 points of coverage — far more than any field choice. Before adding attributes it is worth asking whether the t−6 … t−4 reference window and the MIN_REF gate are right for every family, or whether a shorter reference plus a level-and-change pair would keep more of the book scoreable.

Per-fold stability

Seven folds, AUC standard deviation ~0.017. Every headline number in this report is a pooled figure. The per-fold precision spread has not been examined, and if one origin is carrying the result the conclusion changes.

No live evidence that an alert changes an outcome

Everything here is retrospective. Nothing has been scored forward, no alert has reached a relationship manager, and retained balances — the measure the business would actually judge this on — cannot be produced without a pilot.

6.5 Standing rules this programme has accumulated

Each of these cost a run. They are listed because the next person will otherwise pay for them again.

  • Never quote a pooled accuracy number without its coverage. A metric on the subset a feature can score is not a metric on the book.
  • Fix the cohort before reading a curve across a window. Composition otherwise masquerades as an effect — this is what invalidated the v1 error-versus-k curve and it is why §2.4 reads the restricted cohort twice.
  • Peer groups must be frozen. Recomputed monthly, a shrinking client slides down the deciles alongside its own decline.
  • Ship on the metric that matches the decision. AUC ranked the widest specification first and precision at capacity ranked it fifth.
  • A restated field is not a point-in-time field. segment_desc carries a restatement lift of 21.7 at rel_m −1 and is excluded everywhere.
  • Every id is a string end to end. An int64/string mismatch in 2026-07 typed every counterparty unknown and produced entirely plausible output.
  • Check whether an index is absolute or relative. m_idx runs 24289–24319; treating it as 0-based built 24,296 empty folds and failed silently.
  • A gate designed for one variable type will silently delete another. MIN_REF > 1.0 nulled five feature families and their zero deltas were read as evidence.
  • Give every row in a summary table a distinct label. A duplicate key in a dict printed one count where three were intended, and it survived two runs.
