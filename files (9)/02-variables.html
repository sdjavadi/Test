2 · What we predict it from

Independent variables, the difference-in-differences transform, and what was deliberately left out
Every independent variable is a relative quantity: this client against its own recent history, then against its frozen peer group in the same calendar month. Nothing is used at its raw level. That construction is what lets the model survive a data set with a hard 2024 floor and still be computable in production every month.

2.1 The transform

chg(t) = value(t) / mean( value over t-6 … t-4 )     needs 2+ observations, reference > 1.0
dd(t)  = chg(t) / median( chg(t) across the client's FROZEN peer group, same calendar month )

Two design choices matter more than the formula:

  • The reference window is t−6 … t−4, not a 12-month event-aligned pre-window. A 12-month event-aligned baseline would permanently discard ~4,200 of 16,384 attriters, because nothing before 2024 is trusted and none can be added. The short trailing window survives the floor and is available at scoring time.
  • Peer deciles are frozen from the client's first three observed months and never recomputed. Recomputed monthly, a shrinking client slides down the deciles alongside its own decline and the signal cancels itself out.

The construction check is median_dd = 1.000 for every covered feature, and it holds.

The gate that broke on bounded ratios

reference > 1.0 is correct for counts and amounts and impossible for a share — a quantity in [0, 1] can never have a reference mean above 1.0. Every ratio feature added in v8 came back null on every row. The repair is an additive difference-in-differences for those features: chg = value − reference, dd = chg − peer median(chg), with the sanity check flipping to median_dd = 0.000. Detail in §5.

2.3 Which rail goes first

Six months before the exit, cheque volume is already at 0.220 of the peer-adjusted baseline and RTP at 0.015, while ACH is still at 0.736. Clients keep using ACH while everything else stops. This ordering is one of the most stable findings in the programme and it survived every rebuild.
Payment count collapses from 0.946 to 0.000 while the average ticket holds at 0.968 → 0.616. A departing client does not negotiate its invoices down; it stops sending them through us. That is the clearest available hint that this is displacement rather than contraction — though it is a hint, not a test.
The gap widens monotonically from 0.034 to 0.252 against a stayer line pinned at 0.440–0.456. The collapse after month 0 is mechanical — there is no flow at all once the accounts are gone.

2.4 Four signals with no visible onset

Four features — amt_in_internal, amt_out_check, cpty_new_out, fin_new_out — sat at exactly −12 in v6 and sit at exactly −18 when the window is widened on the same 4,734 clients. The median cohort effect across all eligible features is 0.000, so this is not composition. These four have no detectable onset in a 31-month panel and are standing characteristics of clients who later leave, not triggers.

The control that makes this readable

Only attriters with 18 months of dd-bearing history can enter the widened window, and those are a later, longer-tenured cohort — 4,734 clients, 53.0% of the 12-month set. So the same restricted cohort is read twice: once at −18 and once at the v6 window. Only the within-cohort movement is evidence. This is the trap that invalidated an error-versus-k curve in the counterparty locatability work, and it is not repeated here.

2.5 Deliberately excluded

VariableWhy it is out
segment_descLeakage. It is restated retrospectively rather than being point-in-time — restatement lift of 21.7 at rel_m −1. It is excluded from the peer grouping and from every feature set.
avg_monthly_bal_1 as a within-month signalIt is the prior complete month, refreshed at month end, and it is absent on every sweep leg. Usable as a label input, not as a current-month feature.
maturity_dt, certificate_number, rates, promo codes No CDs exist in this universe — maturity_dt is null throughout. The rate and promo fields are populated but were never in scope; see §3, several are worth revisiting.
Customer attributes — NAICS, size band, product holdings Held back on purpose. They arrive after the payment work is settled, so their lift is measurable against a fixed baseline rather than confounded with a feature rebuild.
