5 · Feature engineering and the ablation

74 new features from data already queryable, a construction defect that nulled five families, and the block-by-block test that decides what ships
Coverage was the constraint v7 identified, so v8 built 74 features aimed at it — inbound counterparties, inbound institutions, recurring-payment breakage, timing, self-payment to an external bank. The ablation says one of those families carries almost the whole gain, several carry nothing, and one — trend — actively destroys the combined model.

5.2 The defect that nulled five families

The dd transform gates on reference > 1.0. That is correct for counts and amounts. A share lives in [0, 1] and can never clear it. Every ratio feature came back dd = null on every row: all the *_retention and *_rec_broken_share terms, conc_top1, conc_top3, railmix_shift, railmix_rails_collapsed, acc_closed_share. acc_live_ratio scraped 1.25%.

The consequence was invisible in the output. The concentration and railmix blocks reported n_feat = 36 — identical to the baseline, because the zero-variance guard dropped every column they contributed. Their 0.0000 deltas were read as "these features don't work". They were never tested.

chg = value − mean(t−6 … t−4)      no MIN_REF gate
dd  = chg  − median(chg over peers)
Centred at 0 rather than 1, so the construction check flips to median_dd = 0.000 — and it holds for all 23. Mean coverage moves from 0.000 to 0.328.
+ everything moves 0.5977 → 0.7103, a gain of 0.1126. Most of that is not the repair — it is the removal of the 94 trend features from the combined specification. Every other block gains between 0.003 and 0.029 from the repair itself.
"Trajectory is free information" was a stated expectation going into v8. It is wrong as specified: a signal minus its own trailing 3-month mean is mostly noise on a dd that is already a difference, and 94 of them swamp the fit. Deleted, not parked.
Precision is flat across a 200-fold change in the ridge penalty (0.7103 → 0.7120 → 0.7097 → 0.7080). Regularisation was not the problem. The v8 collapse was 94 noise features, and once they are gone the wide specification behaves normally.

The wide specifications have the best AUC (0.8031–0.8035) and are beaten on precision by four much smaller ones. AUC averages over the whole ranking; a 250-name list lives in its top 0.3%. Ship on the metric that matches the decision.

The recommended specification

+ fin_in + cpty_acct_out — 84 features, AUC 0.8006, precision at 250 alerts a month 0.7269 against a baseline of 0.6514. That is +0.0754 absolute, 11.6% relative. fin_in alone gets to 0.7246, so the second block is worth a further 0.0023 for 24 more features — arguably not worth carrying if simplicity matters.

A gap before this is briefed

The v8b harness reports precision on client-months. The capacity-matched table in §4 — the one with distinct clients, conversations and lead time — was computed on the v7 specification. The customer-level translation of the 0.7269 has not been run. Until it is, the honest statement is a precision improvement, not a client count. Also: seven folds with an AUC standard deviation of ~0.017 means the per-fold precision spread matters. If one origin is carrying the result, that is a different story, and it has not been checked.
